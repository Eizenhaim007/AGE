# AGE website brand guidelines

The Director’s Cut design is the proposed client-review baseline. Treat the approved version of this document and `src/styles/tokens.css` as the reference for future website changes.

## Identity

Use Africa Global Engineering as the company name and AGE as the compact mark. Retain the AGE blue mark and white lettering. Use AGE blue `#1A4C8B` for the primary action and the palette in `src/styles/tokens.css` for text, focus, backgrounds, and borders. Use Manrope for headings and Inter for body and interface text.

## Content and imagery

Lead with civil engineering and technical consulting in Casablanca, Morocco, and Africa. Use verifiable project and capability descriptions. Label illustrative infrastructure imagery. Use AGE-provided project photography where available. Add photos or case-study links when their attribution and destinations are confirmed. Align service names, project categories, claims, captions, and contact details across French and English.

## Layout and interactions

Keep the primary inquiry action easy to find. Project cards share equal columns, a consistent media ratio, and aligned captions, then stack in one column on small screens. Use consistent radii, a visible focus ring, and 44 px target heights for primary controls. The language switch remains visible on mobile. The site must remain readable with reduced motion and navigable by keyboard.

## Inquiry experience

Show the direct WhatsApp, call, and email options. When the inquiry form is enabled, require a full name, reply method, its matching contact value, and a message. Keep company and project type optional. Preserve values on errors, present feedback beside the relevant field, and use the localized confirmation after acceptance.

Update these guidelines and tokens together when an approved brand decision changes.
